# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want FloatRead collaboration to be safe, respectful and technically honest. Be considerate, welcome constructive disagreement, explain decisions with evidence, respect privacy and credit other people's work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, publishing another person's private information or credentials, deliberate deception about test/release results, and sustained disruption of project collaboration.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers will review reports confidentially, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently remove participants based on severity and repetition.

Maintainers must disclose conflicts of interest, avoid retaliation and document enforcement decisions without exposing private details.
